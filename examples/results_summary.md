# DualCite results summary

Two venue groups compared: **Computational Linguistics (CL)** vs **Information Retrieval (IR)**.

## Corpus overview

- Total papers in corpus: 15,362
- CL papers: 12,479
- IR papers: 2,883
- Papers with country data: 13,894 (90.4%)
- Intra-corpus citations (edges): 13,439
- Total references made (all, including outside the corpus): 628,301
- References resolved within the corpus: 13,439 (2.1%)
- References to works outside the corpus ("other"): 614,862 (97.9%)

> Most references point to works outside the corpus, such as earlier papers or papers of other venues. The in-corpus citation flow below therefore covers only the references that link two papers of the corpus; its shares are more informative than its absolute counts.

## Citation flows between the two communities

*(Only references that resolve to another paper of the corpus; the "other" references above are excluded.)*

| Direction | Citations | % of all intra-corpus citations |
|---|---:|---:|
| CL → CL (within CL) | 11,593 | 86.3% |
| IR → IR (within IR) | 804 | 6.0% |
| CL → IR (cross) | 577 | 4.3% |
| IR → CL (cross) | 465 | 3.5% |

- Total cross-community citations: 1,042 (7.8% of all)
- Cross-flow asymmetry (CL→IR vs IR→CL): 577 vs 465  (ratio 1.24)

## Internal / external / other, matched against all-years lists

Every reference of the corpus papers is matched against the all-years reference lists of **CL** and **IR** and classified as internal (same community), external (other community), or other (in neither list). This measurement covers the full history of both communities.

*(Reference lists: CL: 120,552 titles / 0 DOIs; IR: 35,479 titles / 35,687 DOIs.)*

### References made by CL papers

| Target | References | % |
|---|---:|---:|
| Internal (→ CL, any year) | 148,676 | 28.7% |
| External (→ IR, any year) | 6,655 | 1.3% |
| Other (neither) | 361,820 | 70.0% |
| **Total** | **517,151** | 100% |

- Internal-to-external ratio: 22.3 (CL cites its own community 22.3× more than the other)

| Target | References | % |

### Cross-community comparison (all years)

- CL → IR references: 6,655
- IR → CL references: 10,659
- Asymmetry ratio (CL→IR : IR→CL): 0.62

*Note: the two reference lists may differ in coverage (e.g. one spans an entire anthology, the other a fixed set of venues), so the 'internal' share is more complete for the better-covered community. The cross-community asymmetry is unaffected by this.*
